1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
This policy outlines authorized discount authorizations, credit policies,
and commercial pricing guidelines for all Enterprise Storage and Solution lines.

2. DISCOUNT AUTHORIZATION THRESHOLDS
2.1 Standard Tier Partners:
    - Maximum standard allowable discount without VP approval: 8.0%.
    - Maximum approved discretionary discount: 10.0%.

2.2 Enterprise Tier Partners:
    - Standard authorized discount across all regions (EMEA, NA): 12.0%.
    - Specialized APAC Regional Provision: In accordance with APAC regional expansion
      frameworks, the maximum allowable discount cap is strictly capped at 15.0%.
    - Any transaction granting a discount exceeding 15.0% in the APAC territory
      constitutes an immediate contract violation requiring formal audit review.

3. PAYMENT AND SETTLEMENT TERMS
    - Standard commercial payment timeline: Net 45 days from invoice issuance.
    - Tier-1 Enterprise clients maintain maximum line-of-credit threshold of $150,000 USD.
